================================================================================
  OUTLAST VR - BETA 1.0 TESTER BUILD 2 (2026-10-17)
  VR mod made by StrikeZoneVR
================================================================================

INSTALL
  See README.txt: copy the files into your Outlast folder (Steam -> Outlast ->
  Manage -> Browse local files -> Binaries\Win64), start Virtual Desktop, run
  Outlast1VR.exe and choose PLAY IN VR. Made and tested on a Quest 3 with
  Virtual Desktop.
  Updating from tester build 1: copy everything over the old files. The old
  Outlast-VR.bat is gone - the launcher installs and uninstalls (delete the
  old .bat if it is still in your folder).

CONTROLS
  controls\Outlast1VR_Controls.png   every button
  controls\Outlast1VR_Hands_On.png   what your hands and body do
  (the pictures are only for you - they do not go in the game folder)

NEW IN THIS BUILD (from your feedback)
  - No more freezing when the game loads a new area: "it's hitching quite a
    lot ... it freezes up and lurches ... new room, loading, loading". The
    VR mod made the game wait on the graphics card every time it loaded new
    textures. On a walk through the admin block on our test PC: 136 freezes
    of up to 0.6 s before, none now. Tell us if anything looks wrong with
    the textures (outlastvr.ini [XR] TextureShadow=0 goes back to the old way).
  - Film grain: VR SETTINGS -> "Film grain" (in the game and the launcher).
    Off by default - in a headset the grain reads as blur.
  - VR hands keep working after Trager's wheelchair scene (losing the two
    fingers used to switch them off for the rest of the game).
  - The launcher opens full screen (F11 or Alt+Enter for a window - it
    remembers).
  - The camcorder's battery meter and record light sit further in from the
    edges by default (VR SETTINGS -> "Camcorder display size").
  - Faster: the game's render thread - what holds Outlast back in VR, and
    why the graphics settings made little difference - does about a third
    less work per frame. Measured on our test laptop (RTX 4050): 123 -> 182
    fps and 137 -> 203 fps uncapped in two spots, the same picture. Two
    changes: your MONITOR now shows 30 frames a second while you play (the
    headset still gets every frame - VR SETTINGS -> "Monitor picture" to
    change it), and the game no longer draws and waits for hidden-object
    tests whose answers the mod already overrides.
  - Turning: a new VR SETTINGS choice, "Smooth turn speed" - 60 degrees a
    second up to MAX (about 260, the game's fastest, and the default). If
    turning still feels too slow, tell us when (camcorder up? zoomed in?).
  - Zoom: with the camcorder up, only a clear up or down push on the right
    stick zooms - turning no longer zooms by accident. The zoomed picture
    fills your view with no black bars ("Zoom with black bars" brings the
    frame back if you prefer it). Tell us which you like better.
  - Camcorder display size: a new VR SETTINGS choice. Can't see the battery
    meter or the record light when you look through the camcorder? Make it
    smaller and they move in from the edges.
  - Vaulting works again: run at a low wall or a table and press A. Climbing
    with your hands no longer gets in the way.
  - Stutter every few seconds: the log is now written in the background, so
    writing it can never hold up a frame, and it records the time of every
    frame slower than 40 ms. If you still get drops, the log shows when.
  - Quest Link / Air Link works: the "screen on each eye, distorted" picture
    is fixed - the lens-shaped eyes turn off by themselves on Link.
  - The launcher tells you why when it can't apply the VR settings (e.g.
    Windows ransomware protection blocking it) and finds Outlast's settings
    in more places.
  - The old Outlast-VR.bat is gone from the download.

PLEASE TRY, AND TELL US HOW IT FEELS
  - Turning at a few different "Turn speed" settings, and snap turning.
  - Vaulting and climbing: vault a few tables / low walls, climb a ledge or a
    ladder with your hands.
  - Zooming while you turn.
  - Where it runs slowly, if anywhere (see below).

IF IT STUTTERS
  Keep VR SETTINGS -> "Monitor picture" at 30 FPS (EVERY FRAME is much
  slower). Render resolution 85-90% helps if your graphics card is the
  limit; Outlast's own graphics options make little difference. Tell us
  where in the game it got slow, and send the log - it now says exactly when
  frames were slow.

KNOWN ISSUES
  - Some heavier areas later in the game may still run below the headset's
    frame rate: the game draws everything twice, once for each eye, on one
    CPU core. This build takes a third off that work; tell us where it is
    still slow.
  - Miles's legs do not walk when you take real steps - he slides with you.

UNINSTALLING
  Close Outlast, run Outlast1VR.exe and choose UNINSTALL VR MOD (press it
  twice). It puts the game's own settings back and removes every mod file from
  the game folder; the launcher deletes itself when you close it.

REPORTING A PROBLEM
  Right after playing, close the game and send:
    %LOCALAPPDATA%\OutlastVR\OutlastVR_Log.txt
  (launcher -> VR SETTINGS -> "Open the log folder"), with a short video or a
  description of what happened and where. The log starts over every time the
  game starts, so grab it before you play again.
